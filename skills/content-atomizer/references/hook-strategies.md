# 6 Hook Opening Strategies

Proven first-sentence strategies for social media posts, threads, articles, and emails. Every piece of content should open with one of these strategies.

## Strategy 1: Strong Declarative Sentence
Choose a side and stick to it. Readers who agree will read on for validation, those who disagree will want to know your opinion.
- "Being physically fit isn't a hobby, it's a lifestyle."
- "The wealthiest people have simple investment portfolios."
- "Becoming a successful entrepreneur doesn't just 'happen.'"

## Strategy 2: Thought-Provoking Question
Engage curiosity by asking a question your audience is already wondering about.
- "Is there such a thing as complete happiness?"
- "Did you know the average Millennial has less than $10,000 in savings?"
- "What holds back amateur basketball players from ever making it to pro status?"

## Strategy 3: Controversial Opinion
Challenge the status quo with a confident stance.
- "ChatGPT is overused and overhyped."
- "Becoming wealthy changes your character."
- "95% of reading is procrastination disguised as planning."

## Strategy 4: Moment in Time
Draw readers in by grounding them in a specific scene or event.
- "My fear of bottomless fries began in 4th grade."
- "In 1982, David Ogilvy wrote a memo titled 'How to write.'"
- "At 7:55 AM Hawaii time, December 7th, 1941, America changed forever."

## Strategy 5: Vulnerable Statement
Relate emotionally by sharing personal experiences that connect with your audience.
- "For 27 years, I've struggled with OCD."
- "As a teenager, I was plagued with social anxiety."
- "For the first 10 years of my career, I was a terrible husband."

## Strategy 6: Weird, Unique Insight
Surprise readers with an unexpected fact or stat.
- "Texas is not the largest state in the US. Alaska is."
- "On average, it rains more in Paris than it does in London."
- "Crows are one of the most intelligent animals on planet earth."

## Headline + Hook Combination Formula

Combine one of the 5 headline elements with one of the 6 opening strategies:

**Headline elements:** (1) Be CLEAR, (2) Specify WHO, (3) Specify WHAT, (4) Specify WHY, (5) Twist The Knife

**Example combinations:**
- Moment in Time + Twist The Knife: "In 1982, Ogilvy wrote a memo on writing. These 10 rules still work (and rule #7 will change how you write forever)"
- Controversial Opinion + WHAT: "AI content is killing your brand. Here's why writing like a human is your only competitive advantage left"
- Vulnerable Statement + WHY: "I spent 3 years writing content nobody read. Here's the single shift that took me from 0 to 100K followers"

## Constraints
- Do NOT use "unlock", "unleash", or "imagine"
- Do NOT use hashtags in hooks
- Prioritize quality over speed
- Use the target audience's pain points to attract attention
