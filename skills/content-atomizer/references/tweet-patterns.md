# 20 Tweet Patterns

A comprehensive taxonomy of tweet types. Use when generating X/Twitter content to ensure structural variety. When generating tweets, randomly select from these patterns rather than defaulting to the same format.

## General Advice / Observation
1. **Pithy Statement** — Short, impactful advice or observation. "Your network is your net worth."
2. **Statement + Action** — Advice followed by a call to action. "The best time to start writing was yesterday. The second best time is now. What are you waiting for?"
3. **Open-Ended Question** — Thought-provoking question inviting reflection. "What's the biggest lesson you learned in your 20s?"
4. **Direct Quote** — Quote from a famous figure, lending credibility. "The only way to do great work is to love what you do.' - Steve Jobs"
5. **Quote + Commentary** — Quote followed by your personal interpretation or analysis.

## Tips / Lessons / Insights / Secrets
6. **Listicle (Bullets)** — Numbered tips in concise bullet points for easy consumption.
7. **Listicle (Numbered)** — Numbered list with detailed explanations for each point.
8. **Concept Breakdown** — Specific concept explained in detail with insights, examples, or applications. "The Pareto Principle: 80% of results come from 20% of efforts..."
9. **Concept + Action** — Concise explanation followed by a specific call to action. "Small, consistent actions create massive results. What habit can you start today?"

## Personal Experience / Story
10. **Anecdote + Lesson** — Personal experience illustrating a specific lesson, adding authenticity.
11. **Story Thread** — Longer personal story in multi-tweet thread, building suspense with detailed insights.
12. **Story + Action** — Personal anecdote followed by a relevant call to action.

## Questions / Polls
13. **Multiple Choice Poll** — Multiple-choice question with clearly defined options.
14. **Open-Ended Question** — Question allowing nuanced responses and deeper engagement.
15. **Question + Context** — Question followed by additional context to encourage thoughtful responses.

## Promotions / Announcements
16. **Direct Promotion** — Simple announcement with a direct link.
17. **Promotion + Action** — Announcement followed by a call to action for engagement or lead generation.
18. **Testimonial + Promotion** — Client testimonial followed by a relevant promotion.

## Humorous / Controversial
19. **Humorous Statement + Action** — Humorous or controversial statement designed to spark conversation, followed by a CTA.
20. **Statement + Media** — Humorous or controversial statement paired with a relevant image or video.

## Usage Guidelines
- When generating a batch of tweets, distribute across at least 4 different pattern categories
- Match pattern type to content goal (tips for value, stories for connection, humor for engagement)
- For thread generation, use patterns 6-9 for body tweets, patterns 1-5 for the hook tweet
