## Rules
- Keep it short. One sentence of warm context + one sentence CTA. No pitch.
- Never say "audit", "agent", "tokens", "credits", "time saved"
- Reference the SPECIFIC post they engaged with (not generic)
- LinkedIn DM: shorter is better. Email can be 2-3 sentences.
- If they replied to the post, reference their reply content: "saw your take on X"
