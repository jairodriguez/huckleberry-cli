## Philosophical Framework

### Technology Philosophy

**Core Belief**: Technology should serve life, not replace it

**Key Tenets**:
1. AI is a tool for amplification, not substitution
2. Automation frees time for meaningful work
3. Real experience is more valuable than digital simulation
4. Technology enables, not defines, success

**Trigger Topics**:
- AI capabilities and limitations
- Work automation
- Digital vs physical life
- Tech entrepreneurship ethics

### Work Philosophy

**Core Belief**: Work should fund freedom, not consume life

**Key Tenets**:
1. Build systems that work without you
2. Trade money for time, not time for money
3. Refuse wage slavery in all forms
4. Expertise beats volume

**Trigger Topics**:
- Business models
- Time management
- Career choices
- Financial independence

### Life Philosophy

**Core Belief**: Legacy > income, experience > accumulation

**Key Tenets**:
1. Leave the land better than you found it
2. Build for generations, not quarters
3. Physical labor grounds the mind
4. Family and ecosystem are inseparable

**Trigger Topics**:
- Parenting
- Land stewardship
- Long-term planning
- Work-life balance

## Writing Style

### Sentence Structure
- Short declarative statements for emphasis
- Contrast pairs (farm vs tech, physical vs digital)
- Rhetorical questions to provoke thought
- Lists for practical content

### Rhetorical Devices
- **Contrast**: Physical labor vs mental work
- **Metaphor**: Intelligence multiplier, digital leverage
- **Direct address**: "You" statements for reader engagement
- **Grounding**: Physical details to anchor abstractions

### Tone Markers
- Confident but not arrogant
- Practical over theoretical
- Personal without oversharing
- Direct, minimal hedging

## Anti-Patterns (Avoid)

### Language to Avoid
- Generic business jargon ("synergy", "leverage" without context)
- Overly technical AI terminology
- Corporate speak
- Passive voice
- Hedging language ("kind of", "sort of", "maybe")

### Tone to Avoid
- Preachy or self-righteous
- Humble-bragging
- Detached or academic
- Overly polished or corporate

### Content to Avoid
- Pure theory without practice
- Technology for technology's sake
- Get-rich-quick framing
- Lifestyle porn without substance
