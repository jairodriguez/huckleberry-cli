# AI Slop Detection Patterns

Comprehensive list of artificial-sounding patterns that indicate content generated by AI without proper human refinement. These patterns make content feel generic, corporate, and inauthentic.

---

## Category 1: Corporate Buzzwords

### High-Severity (Always Flag)
- **"leverage"** - Use "use" instead
- **"synergy"** - Be specific about what works together
- **"ecosystem"** - Say "system", "network", or be specific
- **"paradigm shift"** - Describe the actual change
- **"game-changer"** - Explain why it matters specifically
- **"circle back"** - Say "follow up" or "discuss later"
- **"touch base"** - Say "connect", "talk", or "check in"
- **"deep dive"** - Say "detailed look" or "analyze"
- **"drill down"** - Say "examine" or "look closely at"
- **"unpack"** - Say "explain" or "break down"
- **"robust"** - Be specific about capabilities
- **"scalable"** - Explain how it scales
- **"best practices"** - Show the actual practices
- **"thought leadership"** - Demonstrate expertise instead
- **"end-to-end"** - Describe the complete process
- **"holistic"** - Say "complete" or "comprehensive"
- **"streamline"** - Explain how it's simplified
- **"optimize"** - Show specific improvements
- **"bandwidth"** - Say "time" or "capacity"
- **"ping"** - Say "message" or "contact"

### Medium-Severity (Warn)
- **"solution"** (overused) - Be specific about what it solves
- **"platform"** (generic) - Describe what it actually is
- **"framework"** (vague) - Show the structure
- **"approach"** (overused) - Describe the method
- **"strategy"** (generic) - Detail the plan
- **"innovative"** - Show what's new
- **"cutting-edge"** - Prove it's advanced
- **"state-of-the-art"** - Demonstrate superiority
- **"next-generation"** - Explain the improvement
- **"world-class"** - Provide evidence

## Category 2: Excessive Politeness

### Email/DM Patterns
- **"I'd love to hear your thoughts"** → "What do you think?"
- **"I'd be happy to..."** → "I can..." or "Let me..."
- **"Please let me know"** → "Let me know" or "Tell me"
- **"If you could..."** → "Can you..."
- **"Would you mind..."** → "Could you..."
- **"I hope this helps"** → Remove (implicit)
- **"Feel free to..."** → "You can..." or direct instruction
- **"Don't hesitate to..."** → "Just..." or direct instruction

### Social Media Patterns
- **"I'd love to hear from you in the comments"** → "Drop your experience below"
- **"Please share your thoughts"** → "What's your take?"
- **"I'd be curious to know"** → "Which one works for you?"
- **"Let me know what you think"** → "Think I'm wrong? Prove it."

## Category 3: Formal Transitions

### Addition Transitions
- **"Furthermore"** → "Also", "Plus", or new sentence
- **"Additionally"** → "And", "Also", or remove
- **"Moreover"** → Remove or start new paragraph
- **"In addition"** → "Also" or restructure
- **"What's more"** → Remove or use "And"

### Conclusion Transitions
- **"In conclusion"** → Remove (structure shows conclusion)
- **"To summarize"** → Remove or "Here's what matters:"
- **"In essence"** → Remove or "Simply:"
- **"Ultimately"** → "The result:" or remove
- **"At the end of the day"** → Remove or specific outcome

### Contrast Transitions
- **"However"** → "But", or new sentence with contrast
- **"Nevertheless"** → "But still" or "Even so"
- **"Nonetheless"** → "Still" or remove
- **"On the contrary"** → "Actually" or remove
- **"Conversely"** → "Instead" or restructure

### Example Transitions
- **"For instance"** → "Like:" or just show example
- **"For example"** → Remove and show example
- **"Such as"** → "like" or list examples
- **"Namely"** → ":" or restructure

## Category 4: Generic Enthusiasm

### Excitement Markers
- **"Amazing!"** → Describe why it's good specifically
- **"Incredible!"** → Show the impressive part
- **"Awesome!"** → Be specific about value
- **"Fantastic!"** → Detail what's good
- **"Wonderful!"** → Explain the benefit
- **"Excellent!"** → Show excellence

### Announcement Patterns
- **"Excited to share!"** → Remove, just share
- **"Thrilled to announce..."** → Remove, just announce
- **"Happy to present..."** → Remove, just present
- **"Delighted to introduce..."** → Remove, just introduce
- **"Proud to showcase..."** → Remove, just showcase
- **"Can't wait to..."** → Remove or "Coming:" with date
- **"Stay tuned!"** → Specific date/time or remove
- **"Coming soon!"** → Give actual date or remove
- **"More to come!"** → Be specific about what and when

### Gratitude Overuse
- **"Thank you so much for..."** (in content) → One "thanks" is enough
- **"I really appreciate..."** (in content) → Show appreciation through value
- **"Huge thanks to..."** (every post) → Occasional only

## Category 5: Hedge Language

### Uncertainty Markers
- **"I think"** → State directly or remove
- **"Perhaps"** → Be definitive or explain uncertainty
- **"Maybe"** → Choose or explain options
- **"Possibly"** → Commit or explain variables
- **"Probably"** → Be specific about likelihood
- **"Potentially"** → Show potential or remove
- **"It seems"** → Verify or state clearly
- **"Appears to be"** → Confirm or specify
- **"Could be"** → Investigate or state
- **"Might"** → Be more certain or explain conditions
- **"May"** → Be specific about conditions
- **"Somewhat"** → Quantify or remove
- **"Rather"** → Be definitive
- **"Fairly"** → Quantify specifically
- **"Quite"** → Remove or be specific

### Qualification Softeners
- **"In my opinion"** → Implied, remove
- **"I believe"** → State directly
- **"It's my understanding that"** → "It is" or verify
- **"From my perspective"** → Implied, remove
- **"As far as I know"** → Verify or state clearly
- **"To be honest"** → Implies dishonesty otherwise, remove
- **"To be frank"** → Just be frank
- **"Generally speaking"** → Be specific or remove
- **"For the most part"** → Quantify or remove

## Category 6: Passive Voice Overuse

### Passive Construction Patterns
- **"It can be seen that..."** → "You'll see..."
- **"It should be noted that..."** → Remove, just note it
- **"It is believed that..."** → "We believe" or "X believes"
- **"It has been found that..."** → "Studies found" or "X found"
- **"It is recommended that..."** → "I recommend" or "Do X"
- **"It is important to..."** → "You should..." or "Do..."
- **"It is suggested that..."** → "Try..." or "Do..."
- **"It is worth noting that..."** → Remove and note it

### Agency Removal
- **"Mistakes were made"** → "I/We made mistakes"
- **"The decision was made"** → "X decided"
- **"Results were achieved"** → "We achieved results"
- **"Progress has been made"** → "We've made progress"

---

## Category 7: Vague Quantifiers

### Imprecise Numbers
- **"Many"** → Give exact number or range
- **"Several"** → Specify how many
- **"Various"** → List them
- **"Numerous"** → Count them
- **"Countless"** → Estimate or acknowledge limitation
- **"Significant"** → Quantify the significance
- **"Substantial"** → Show the size
- **"Considerable"** → Measure it
- **"A lot of"** → Count or estimate

### Vague Time References
- **"Soon"** → Give date/timeframe
- **"Later"** → When specifically?
- **"Recently"** → When exactly?
- **"In the near future"** → Specify timeline
- **"Shortly"** → Give timeframe
- **"Eventually"** → When?
- **"Ongoing"** → Since when? Until when?

### Vague Quality Descriptors
- **"Good"** → Define criteria
- **"Better"** → Better how? By what measure?
- **"Improved"** → Improved in what way? By how much?
- **"Enhanced"** → What enhancement?
- **"Optimized"** → Optimized how?
- **"Efficient"** → Efficient compared to what?
- **"Effective"** → Define effectiveness metric

- **"Win-win situation"** → Explain benefits to each party
- **"At the end of the day"** → Remove or "Ultimately"
- **"Take it to the next level"** → Describe improvement

- **"Get the ball rolling"** → "Start by..."
- **"Keep your eye on the ball"** → "Focus on X"
- **"Step up to the plate"** → "Take responsibility for X"

### Achievement Clichés

- **"Pushing the envelope"** → Explain innovation
- **"Breaking new ground"** → Detail the breakthrough
- **"Paving the way"** → Show leadership
- **"Raising the bar"** → Quantify improvement

### Tech/AI Clichés (2024-2025)
- **"Unlock the power of..."** → "Use X to do Y"
- **"Harness the potential of..."** → "X enables you to..."
- **"Transform your..."** → Show specific changes
- **"Revolutionize..."** → Explain actual change
- **"Supercharge your..."** → Quantify improvement
- **"Take control of..."** → "Manage X by..."
- **"Master the art of..."** → "Get good at X by..."

## Category 9: Filler Words & Phrases

### Content Padding
- **"It goes without saying"** → Then don't say it
- **"Needless to say"** → Don't say it
- **"It is worth mentioning"** → Just mention it
- **"As you may know"** → Assume they don't, explain
- **"As previously mentioned"** → Reference or remove
- **"In other words"** → Pick better words first time
- **"That being said"** → Restructure without
- **"Having said that"** → Remove and make point
- **"All things considered"** → Consider them, don't announce it
- **"When all is said and done"** → Skip to conclusion

### Redundant Pairs
- **"Each and every"** → "Each" or "every"
- **"First and foremost"** → "First" or "most important"
- **"Any and all"** → "All"
- **"Hopes and dreams"** → "Goals"
- **"Tips and tricks"** → "Tips" or "tactics"
- **"Ways and means"** → "Methods"
- **"Pick and choose"** → "Choose"

---

## Category 10: Question Patterns

### Rhetorical Questions (Overused)
- **"So what does this mean?"** → Just explain it
- **"What's the takeaway?"** → Just give takeaway
- **"Why does this matter?"** → Just show why
- **"How can you apply this?"** → Just show application
- **"What can we learn from this?"** → Just share lesson

### Good Question Patterns (Keep These)
- Direct engagement: "Which one worked for you?"
- Specific debate: "Think AI will replace X? Why?"
- Experience sharing: "Ever tried this?"
- Tactical choice: "Option A or B for your use case?"

---

## Category 11: Listicle Intro Phrases

### Generic List Intros (Overused)
- **"Here are X ways to..."** → Just list them
- **"In this post, I'll share..."** → Just share it
- **"Today, we're going to explore..."** → Just explore
- **"Let's dive into..."** → Just dive in
- **"Without further ado..."** → Remove completely
- **"So let's get started..."** → Already started

### Better Alternatives
- Hook with outcome: "5 mistakes costing you $50K+"
- Lead with problem: "Your automation fails because..."
- Start with claim: "Product-ified services beat agencies. Here's why:"
- Open with question: "Why do 80% of AI agencies fail?"

---

## Category 12: Call-to-Action Patterns

### Weak CTAs (Generic)
- **"Let me know your thoughts in the comments"** → Specific question
- **"Share this if you found it helpful"** → Reason to share
- **"Follow for more content like this"** → Value promise
- **"Hit the like button"** → Remove (begging)
- **"Subscribe to my newsletter"** → What they'll get
- **"Check out the link in bio"** → What's at link?

### Strong CTAs (Specific)
- **"Drop your stack below"** - Action + topic
- **"Which model fits your niche? Reply."** - Specific question
- **"RT if FIXED > CUSTOM resonates"** - Tie to content
- **"Grab the playbook → [link]"** - Clear offer
- **"Try this today and tell me what breaks"** - Action + engagement

## Detection Strategy

### Automated Scanning
1. **Word frequency analysis** - Flag words appearing >2x in same piece
2. **Phrase matching** - Check against pattern library
3. **Passive voice ratio** - Flag if >20% of sentences
4. **Question frequency** - Flag if >3 rhetorical questions
5. **Transition density** - Flag excessive formal transitions

### Manual Review
1. **Read aloud test** - Does it sound like a human talking?
2. **Specificity check** - Can vague terms be replaced with specifics?
3. **Voice match** - Does it match brand voice profile?
4. **Engagement test** - Would you stop scrolling for this?

### Severity Scoring
- **High (3 points)**: Corporate buzzwords, generic enthusiasm
- **Medium (2 points)**: Excessive politeness, formal transitions
- **Low (1 point)**: Minor hedge language, filler words

**Total Score Interpretation**:
- 0-3 points: Clean, authentic voice
- 4-7 points: Needs polish
- 8-12 points: Significant AI slop, major revision
- 13+ points: Start over, too generic

## Brand-Specific Exceptions

### withjai-weekly (Farm-to-Founder Voice)
**Allowed "AI slop" in context**:
- "Amazing" - Only when describing genuinely surprising results
- "Excited" - Only in product announcements
- "Game-changer" - Only with proof (revenue impact, time savings)

**Encouraged patterns**:
- Conversational asides: "Sounds wild—until..."
- Direct commands: "Drop yours below"
- Bold claims: "FIXED > CUSTOM"
- No em-dashes (strict rule)

### enchantents (Luxury Hospitality Voice)
**Allowed formal language**:
- "Exceptional" - Appropriate for luxury positioning
- "Transform" - Guest experience transformation is key messaging
- "Elevated" - Premium positioning word

**Avoid casualizing**:
- Keep professional tone (not too casual)
- "Amazing" is okay for guest experiences
- More formal than withjai-weekly (intentional)

### xelite-one (Premium Coaching Voice)
**Allowed authority language**:
- "Transform" - Core messaging for coaching
- "Leverage" - In context of assets/advantages
- "Framework" - When describing methodology

**Avoid softening**:
- No hedge language (undermines authority)
- Bold claims supported by proof
- Direct, confident statements

## Replacement Suggestions by Context

### Technical Content
- ❌ "Leverage the API" → ✅ "Use the API"
- ❌ "Optimize your workflow" → ✅ "Cut your workflow from 40 to 4 hours"
- ❌ "Robust solution" → ✅ "Handles 10K requests/second"

### Business Content
- ❌ "Game-changing approach" → ✅ "Increased revenue 4x in 6 months"
- ❌ "Streamline operations" → ✅ "Reduced costs by 30%"
- ❌ "Paradigm shift" → ✅ "From custom → fixed deliverables"

### Social Content
- ❌ "Amazing insights!" → ✅ "These 5 models generate $40K+ monthly"
- ❌ "Excited to share" → ✅ [Just share it]
- ❌ "Let me know your thoughts" → ✅ "Which one fits your niche?"
