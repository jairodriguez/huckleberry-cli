# PAIPS Framework

A 5-step copy framework for persuasive short-form content. Use for tweets, short-form video scripts, and carousel copy where you need agitation before the solution.

## The 5 Steps

### 1. Problem — Highlight something painful
The first line highlights a painful problem that people experience. This line is intended to elicit a strong emotional response and get the reader to the next line.

### 2. Agitate — Agitate the pain
Next, get under the reader's skin and make them agitated. Once someone is agitated, they are much more likely to be open to a solution.

### 3. Intrigue — Introduce something intriguing
Introduce something that makes them think, "Wait, what's that?" Get the audience to lean in with interest to carry them through the content.

### 4. Positive Future — Paint a positive future
Paint a picture of what a positive future would look like. The positive future is tied to something intriguing.

### 5. Solution — Present your solution
Introduce the solution which should be tied to the problem.

## Usage in Scripts

When using PAIPS for a slideshow script:
- Slides 1-2: Problem + Agitate (establish the pain)
- Slide 3: Intrigue (the "wait, what?" moment)
- Slides 4-5: Positive Future + Solution (deliver the payoff)

## Writing Constraints (from PAIPS source)
- Break up sentences longer than 25 words
- Hook sentence must answer: "How will this help me?" or "What will I learn?" or "Is this worth my time?"
- Mix 3-4 short (7-15 word) sentences with one longer (15-25 word) sentence
- Use transition words: However, Anyway, More than that, But
- Use bullets when applicable
- Short, simple, easy-to-read sentences
- Do not repeat concepts
- Do not use "unleash", "unlock", or "imagine"
