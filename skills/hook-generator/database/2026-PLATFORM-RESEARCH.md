## LinkedIn (B2B Professional)

### Algorithm Changes (Early 2026)
**Major Shifts**:
1. **Link Penalty**: Posts with external links get **60% less reach** vs identical posts without links
2. **Comments > Likes**: Post with 50 comments outperforms post with 500 likes
3. **Engagement Bait Detection**: Phrases like "Agree or disagree?" and "What would you add?" are now penalized

**Sources**:
- [The 2026 LinkedIn Algorithm: What Actually Works Right Now](https://rivereditor.com/blogs/2026-linkedin-algorithm-what-works-300-posts-tested)
- [LinkedIn's 2026 Algorithm: The Engagement Bait Era Is Finally Over](https://synergist-digital-media.ghost.io/linkedins-2026-algorithm-the-engagement-bait-era-is-finally-over/)

### Top-Performing Formats
1. **Text-only posts**: Most reach per follower (no image penalty)
2. **PDF carousels**: Highest engagement (keeps users on LinkedIn)
3. **Video clips (30-90s)**: Thriving (platform-native engagement)
4. **Image posts**: 30% LESS reach than text-only (underperforming in 2026)

### Hook Requirements
**Critical**: First 2 lines determine engagement (before "see more" cutoff)

**Top Hook Types**:
1. **First-Person Witness**: "I watched [person] do [surprising thing]..."
 - Example: "I watched Kip Bodnar build an AI app live in under 10 minutes." (81/100 score)
 - Why: Personal, credible, creates FOMO

2. **Surprising Statistics**: "I analyzed 10,000 [things]. [X%] had [finding]."
 - Creates credibility + curiosity gap

3. **Problem-Solution Teaser**: Identifies pain point, promises framework
 - Example: "Most business owners don't know if SEO makes them money. Here's how I track..."

### Validated Performance (Real Data)
**Post 1** (SEO Lead Tracking):
- Hook: "How I track SEO leads & make sure SEO is ROI positive"
- Engagement: 172 likes, 123 comments, 4 shares
- Why: Tackled widespread pain point with framework promise

**Post 2** (AI Skills):
- Hook: "AI skills will be crucial for years to come. Knowing how to use ChatGPT isn't enough."
- Engagement: 1,847 likes, 321 comments, 285 shares
- Why: Fear-based motivation + save-worthy visual reference

**Source**: [Anatomy of a Viral LinkedIn B2B Post](https://gracker.ai/blog/viral-linkedin-b2b-post-anatomy)

### Posting Cadence
- **Optimal**: 3-4 high-quality posts/week
- **Timing**: 7-9 AM audience timezone (fastest initial engagement)
- **Days**: Tuesday-Thursday > Monday/Friday

### What to Avoid
- Engagement pods (detected and penalized)
- Overly promotional content (suppressed)
- Generic engagement bait phrases
- External links in posts (60% reach penalty)

## Instagram Reels (Visual/Creator)

### Algorithm Priorities (2026)
**Ranking Factors**:
1. **Completion rate** (15-60 second sweet spot)
2. **Saves/shares > likes** (high-intent actions prioritized)
3. **First 3 seconds** = critical hook window

### Top Audio Trends (Jan 2026)
**#1: "Every Breath You Take"** (812K Reels)
- Pattern: Emotional, nostalgic reflection + milestone moments
- Works with: Slow transitions, close-ups, sentimental reveals

**#2: "I Wanna Thank Me"** (18K Reels)
- Pattern: Self-empowerment + celebration of personal wins
- Best for: Before-after transformations, achievement showcases

### December 2025 Standouts
**"Twinkling Lights (Reimagined)"** (1.9M Reels) - *Top Performer*
- Hook: Soft sparkle atmosphere without oversaturation
- Strategy: Minimal movement, steady framing, let light carry narrative

**"Turn the Lights Off"** (310K Reels)
- Hook: Builds anticipation through contrast (stillness → motion)
- Pattern: Viewers stay waiting for shift

### Visual Patterns That Work
**What's Winning**:
- Slow transitions > quick cuts (for emotional content)
- Steady framing > aggressive camera movement (reflective content)
- Natural light + textures drive engagement
- Intentional pacing > polish (authenticity beats overproduction)

**What's Underperforming**:
- Fast edits for emotional audio
- Overly polished, corporate-feeling content

### Hook Formulas (Text Overlays)
Based on trending patterns:
1. "This feels illegal to know."
2. "I've been keeping a secret..."
3. "You're not going to believe what I just did/found out."
4. "MYTH: [common belief]"
5. "I was wrong..."

## YouTube (Long-Form Video)
**First 8 seconds critical** (viewer decides to stay or leave)

1. **Problem Reveal**: "The biggest mistake [audience] make is..."
2. **Bold Promise**: "By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to..."
3. **Pattern Interrupt**: Show unexpected result first, then explain

### Title Patterns
- Specificity > vagueness ("16 talking points" not "many tips")
- Numbers work (lists, timeframes, results)
- Keyword at beginning for search

## Cross-Platform Insights

### Universal Patterns (Work Everywhere)
1. **Specificity beats vagueness**: "16 talking points in 10 minutes" > "lots of content quickly"
2. **First-person > third-person**: "I watched..." > "Kip Bodnar did..."
3. **Curiosity gaps**: Promise reveal ("Here's the framework:")
4. **Emotional triggers**: Surprise, opportunity, concern (NOT generic excitement)
5. **Contrarian angles**: Challenge common beliefs

### What's Dead in 2026
- "Most people think..." (AI cliché)
- Generic promises ("Save time", "Make money")
- Ranges instead of specifics ("10-35 hours")
- Engagement bait phrases
- Overly promotional content
