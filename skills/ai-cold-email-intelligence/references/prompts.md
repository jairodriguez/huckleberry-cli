# David Yu Cold Email Prompts & Frameworks

## The "Nerve" Opener (Phase 5)
"Saw the reviews on [Site] about [Functional Frustration]. I might have a fix for [Problem]."

**Example**:
"Saw the Trustpilot reviews about shipping delays at [Company]. I've helped [Competitor] automate their warehouse tracking so that never happens. Worth a quick look?"

## The "Peer-to-Peer" Rule
Write like you're texting a smart colleague. 
- No "Hope you're well"
- No "Best-in-class"
- Use "fix," "problem," "help," "look," "quick."

## The "Yu" Grading Prompt
"Grade this email from 0-100 based on the David Yu System. 
A 100/100 email:
1. Mentions a specific customer complaint found in research.
2. Is under 40 words.
3. Has no AI jargon (leverage, synergy, em-dashes).
4. Asks for 'interest' (curious? worth a look?) not 'time' (30 minutes?)."
